Get started with Muse Hub

How do I set up a Muse specialist?

Choose a specialist for your task and download its kit. Use its instructions to set up a separate Muse assistant with a Main chat and three focused subchats.

Follow the assistant setup guide

What can I build with a Muse Space?

Use Planner to organize projects, next actions, and routines. Choose Task Board to move work from To do to Done, or Daily Desk to keep today’s notes and tasks together.

Explore the Space templates

Can two Muse assistants communicate?

Family Link lets you connect two Muse personal assistants so they can exchange requests and results through a connection you configure. Each assistant keeps its own notes and preferences.
